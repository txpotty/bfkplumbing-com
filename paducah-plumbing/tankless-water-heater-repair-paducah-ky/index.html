    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Paducah

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Paducah

    Commercial tankless water heaters in Paducah face heavier usage and require durable repair solutions. Whether it is for a restaurant, hotel or office building, we understand the importance of maintaining hot water availability. Our commercial tankless water heater repair services address larger capacity units, complex manifold systems and often more severe scaling issues. We prioritize fast, efficient service to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Paducah

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Paducah, KY

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Paducah

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your daily routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

BFK Plumbing offers expert electric tankless water heater repair in Paducah, KY, addressing electrical faults, ensuring proper wiring and replacing faulty elements to restore your hot water supply safely and efficiently.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Paducah, KY

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.
