    Water Meter Leak Testing in Owensboro

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in Owensboro

    Leaks in outdoor plumbing, irrigation systems or main water lines buried in your yard can lead to wasted water and landscaping damage. We use ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection to locate buried pipes and identify leaks without extensive digging. Our approach minimizes disruption to your lawn and garden, protecting your property's appearance. Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a serious concern for homes in Owensboro, KY.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Owensboro

    BFK Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology to provide the most accurate and non-invasive service possible in Owensboro. This includes high-resolution thermal cameras for detecting temperature changes caused by hidden moisture, sensitive acoustic sensors that listen for the subtle sounds of escaping water, and video pipe inspection cameras for internal pipe diagnostics. These tools allow us to find leaks with minimal disruption to your property.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Owensboro, KY

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Owensboro, KY

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Owensboro

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore proper watering for your landscape.
