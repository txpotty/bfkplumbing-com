    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Owensboro

    The cost of toilet replacement in Owensboro, KY typically ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the difficulty of the installation.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location generally does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    It is possible for handy homeowners, but old toilets are heavy and flange problems are common once the toilet is pulled. A pro replacement avoids cracked porcelain, leaks and damaged floors.

    Frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain, constant repairs, rocking that returns after resetting and very high water use are common signs. Toilets more than 25 years old are usually good candidates for replacement.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced individually, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    We recommend reliable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber. Each offers a range of styles and features, from basic models to advanced low-flow or comfort height options. We can help you select the right fit for your needs.

    The 'rough-in' measurement is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe. Common rough-ins are 10, 12, or 14 inches. It is crucial for selecting a new toilet that fits correctly without extensive changes to your bathroom plumbing.
