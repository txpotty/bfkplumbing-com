    Shower Valve Replacement and Upgrades in Owensboro

    A leaky or malfunctioning shower valve can waste water and cause temperature inconsistencies.     Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Owensboro

    Replacing an old tub with a walk-in shower improves safety and accessibility. We remove the tub, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. We can add a handheld shower and grab bar blocking. This upgrade is a common choice for aging in place.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Owensboro, KY

    Tile showers can fail when grout cracks or waterproofing breaks down. We handle the plumbing side of tile shower replacement, including valve, drain and pan.     Shower Valve Replacement in Owensboro

    Old two- and three-handle shower valves often leak and lack anti-scald protection. We open the wall from the back or front, remove the old valve and install a modern pressure-balance or thermostatic valve. Where possible, we use a larger trim plate to cover the old opening. This upgrade improves safety and everyday comfort.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Owensboro, KY

    If the valve is sound, replacing the showerhead and trim can refresh the look without opening the wall.
