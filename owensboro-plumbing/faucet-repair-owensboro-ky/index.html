    Disc Faucet Repair in Owensboro

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Owensboro bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Owensboro

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, ensuring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Owensboro, KY

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Owensboro, KY

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it difficult to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer skilled repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Owensboro

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in Owensboro

    The cost of faucet repair in Owensboro, KY typically ranges from $150 to $400, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required. Minor repairs like replacing a washer or O-ring are on the lower end, while more involved issues such as cartridge replacement or internal valve repairs may cost more. We provide a clear, upfront estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in Kentucky homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to expedite repairs.

    If the faucet body is solid and parts are available, a repair is generally worth it. If it is corroded, cracked or discontinued, a new faucet is often the better choice.

    While some minor leaks, like a simple washer replacement on a compression faucet, can be a DIY project, most modern faucets involve specific tools and delicate parts like cartridges or ceramic discs. Incorrect repairs can lead to further damage, more significant leaks, or voided warranties. It's often best to call a professional for a proper and lasting fix.

    Most repairs take 30 to 60 minutes. If a special part must be ordered, we may need a second short visit.

    Low pressure at one faucet is usually a clogged aerator or cartridge. Low pressure everywhere could mean a failing pressure regulator, a supply issue or old corroded pipes.

    We repair Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard, Grohe, Hansgrohe and nearly all other brands. We carry common cartridges on the truck.

    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges.
