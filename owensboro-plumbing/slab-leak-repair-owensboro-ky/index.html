    Slab Leak Reroute in Owensboro

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Owensboro, KY

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Detection in Owensboro

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Owensboro, KY

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Owensboro, KY.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Owensboro, KY

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.
