    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Owensboro

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it will hold.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Owensboro, KY

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. BFK Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Owensboro

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Owensboro dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Owensboro, KY

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Crawlspaces and basements are both common in Kentucky, and crawlspace homes keep most plumbing repairs easier to reach. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Owensboro

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or extensive tree root intrusion.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Owensboro, KY

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Owensboro.
