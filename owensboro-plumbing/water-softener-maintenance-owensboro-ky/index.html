    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Owensboro

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Owensboro, KY usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required. Factors like cleaning a heavily fouled brine tank or replacing minor worn parts can influence the final price. We provide a detailed quote before starting any work.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, restoring your softener's function.

    Once a year is a good schedule for most homes. High hardness and heavy water use can warrant service every six to nine months.

    For the best performance and longevity, we recommend professional water softener maintenance annually. In areas with extremely hard water, or for older systems, a twice-yearly check-up might be worthwhile to prevent issues before they become serious.

    While maintenance itself doesn't usually extend the warranty period, most manufacturers require regular maintenance to keep your warranty valid. Neglecting maintenance can void your warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs. Always check your specific warranty terms.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can signal resin breakdown.

    Look for spots on dishes, scale on faucets, stiff laundry or soap that will not lather. Salt that never goes down in the brine tank is another telling sign.
