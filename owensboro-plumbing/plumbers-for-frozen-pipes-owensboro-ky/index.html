    Burst Pipe Repair in Owensboro, KY

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX connections. Leaks often appear only after the ice melts, sometimes hours later. We shut off the water, open the wall or ceiling only where needed and replace the damaged section. Every repair is pressure tested so you know the line is solid.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Owensboro

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and suggest draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can set that up.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Owensboro, KY

    Beyond immediate repairs, BFK Plumbing offers long-term solutions for preventing frozen pipes in Owensboro, KY. These systems provide consistent, gentle warmth, preventing ice formation even during extended periods of freezing temperatures, offering a reliable defense against costly bursts.
