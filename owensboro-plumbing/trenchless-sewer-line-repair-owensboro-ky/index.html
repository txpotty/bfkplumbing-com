    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Owensboro

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Owensboro homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
