    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Owensboro

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Owensboro, KY can range from $150 to $800, depending on the complexity of the issue and the parts required.     Following the label instructions to relight a pilot is usually safe. If the pilot will not stay lit, or you smell gas, stop and call a licensed plumber.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't sense heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    In most cases, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. If the repair involves major changes to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Owensboro building department might be necessary.
