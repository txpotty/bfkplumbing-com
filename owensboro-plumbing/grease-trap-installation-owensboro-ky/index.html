    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Owensboro

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BFK Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Owensboro for the best efficiency.

    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Owensboro, KY

    While less common, some large residential kitchens or homes with extensive outdoor cooking setups may benefit from a small grease interceptor. These are typically installed under the sink and help prevent fats, oils, and grease from clogging household drains. BFK Plumbing can evaluate your home's needs and provide a suitable residential grease trap installation solution that protects your plumbing system from FOG accumulation.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Owensboro

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Owensboro. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Owensboro

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Owensboro, KY

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are usually a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection.     In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Owensboro, KY

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Much of Kentucky has karst terrain with limestone bedrock and sinkholes, which can slow down sewer line and drainage work. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.
