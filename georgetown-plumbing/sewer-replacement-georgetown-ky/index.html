    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Georgetown

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Georgetown homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Georgetown, KY

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Georgetown

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    A collapsed sewer line can cause sewage backups into your home. Call (502) 300-9896 for emergency service. We inspect, locate the break and plan repairs fast. Temporary measures may restore flow until replacement. Quick response limits damage.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Georgetown

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.
