    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Georgetown

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating.     Burner Cleaning and Repair in Georgetown, KY

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed.     Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Georgetown
