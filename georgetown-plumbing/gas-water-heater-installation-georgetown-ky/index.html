    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Georgetown

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is usually supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and ensure correct setup for your fuel source.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Choosing the right size depends on your household's peak hot water demand, determined by the number of residents and hot water fixtures. Our technicians will assess your needs during the consultation to recommend the appropriate gallon capacity or flow rate for your Georgetown home.
