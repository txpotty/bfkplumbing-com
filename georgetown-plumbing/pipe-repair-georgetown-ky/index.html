    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Georgetown

    Older homes in Georgetown frequently have galvanized steel or aging copper pipes that are prone to corrosion, leading to discolored water, low water pressure and frequent leaks. BFK Plumbing provides full corroded pipe replacement services.     PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Georgetown, KY

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your system.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Georgetown

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Georgetown

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, often used in newer construction and remodels in Georgetown. Our team is proficient in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also offer PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.
