    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Georgetown

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Georgetown, KY typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.     Repeated error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is typically the better value.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement usually takes between 4 and 8 hours. This timeframe can vary based on whether we are replacing an existing tankless unit with a similar model or upgrading from a traditional tank water heater. A tank conversion may require more extensive changes to gas lines, electrical or venting.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater usually lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in hard water areas.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include frequent error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming frequent and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.

    Most jurisdictions in Kentucky require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.
