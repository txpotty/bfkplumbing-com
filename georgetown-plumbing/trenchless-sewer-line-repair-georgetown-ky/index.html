    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Georgetown, KY

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kentucky homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Georgetown, KY

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Georgetown

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Georgetown homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
