    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Ashland

    Residential hydro jetting in Ashland typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to fully scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    For most homes in Ashland, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and damage fragile pipe.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    BFK Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from established manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include frequently recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often point to a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.
