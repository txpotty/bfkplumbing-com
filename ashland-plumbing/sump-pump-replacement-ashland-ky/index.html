    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Ashland

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps usually cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    It depends on the home. Homes with basements, below-grade rooms, high groundwater or drainage pits usually do. River flooding and saturated ground can cause sewer backups, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worthwhile upgrades.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe, proper and effective installation.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements typically do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves major changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary.
