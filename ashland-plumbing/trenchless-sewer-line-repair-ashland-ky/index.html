    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Ashland

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Ashland, KY

    While the upfront cost of trenchless repair might sometimes seem higher than traditional excavation, it often becomes more cost-effective when you count the savings on landscaping, driveway or foundation repair. For many Ashland, KY homeowners, avoiding extensive property restoration makes trenchless the more sensible long-term choice.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Ashland

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Ashland homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Ashland

    Before any trenchless repair, we conduct a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This crucial step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Ashland property, avoiding guesswork.

    Pipe Bursting in Ashland, KY

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Ashland, KY

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be used to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Ashland, KY. This preserves your home's interior and minimizes renovation costs.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Ashland, KY

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Ashland

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
