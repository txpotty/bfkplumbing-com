    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Ashland

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Ashland. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. BFK Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Ashland, KY

    Irrigation systems in Ashland, KY are a frequent source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply.
