    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Ashland, KY

    Persistent toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Ashland, KY homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper. While a plunger can clear minor blockages, persistent clogs indicate a deeper issue in the trap, drain line, or even the main sewer line. Our plumbers use professional-grade augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs without damaging your toilet or pipes. We also inspect for potential causes like root intrusion or pipe corrosion, which are sometimes seen in older homes.

    Running Toilet Repair in Ashland

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers promptly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. We focus on replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.
