    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Ashland, KY

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Ashland

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Ashland, KY

    Many homes in Ashland, KY feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected. Issues like faulty diverter valves, shared drain clogs, or a leaky tub spout can affect the entire unit. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing and repairing problems specific to these combination fixtures, ensuring both your tub and shower work properly. We handle all components, from the faucet to the overflow drain.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Ashland

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can promptly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to ensure a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Ashland

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Ashland, KY

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure if needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Ashland

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, providing proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Ashland, KY

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in adjoining rooms.
