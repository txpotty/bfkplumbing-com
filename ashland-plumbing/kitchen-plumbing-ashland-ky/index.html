    Persistent kitchen sink clogs are often caused by a buildup of grease, food particles, soap scum, and hair in the drain line. Hard water can also contribute to mineral deposits that narrow pipes. Using chemical drain cleaners can sometimes make the problem worse or damage pipes.

    Common signs include a humming noise without grinding, leaks under the sink, foul odors, frequent clogging, or the unit not turning on at all. If you notice any of these, it's best to call a professional to prevent further damage.

    Most food service businesses are required to have a grease trap or interceptor under local pretreatment rules. We can size and install one to fit your kitchen.

    For commercial kitchens in Ashland, grease traps should usually be cleaned every 1 to 3 months, depending on the volume of use and local regulations. For residential use, if you have a grease trap, annual inspection and cleaning are usually sufficient.
