    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Louisville, KY

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it hard to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer expert repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Louisville

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems. Our plumbers are skilled at repairing all kitchen faucet styles, including pull-down, pull-out, and commercial-style fixtures.     Dripping Faucet Repair in Louisville

    A dripping spout usually means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Louisville

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Louisville bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Louisville

We carry a wide range of common parts so most leaky faucet repairs can be completed in a single visit, restoring your peace and quiet.
