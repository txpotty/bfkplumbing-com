    Slab Leak Reroute in Louisville

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Louisville, KY

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but requires skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Louisville, KY.

    Slab Leak Detection in Louisville

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Louisville, KY

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose significant health risks and can cause extensive damage.
