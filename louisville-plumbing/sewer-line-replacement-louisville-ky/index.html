    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Louisville, KY

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Louisville and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Louisville

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.
