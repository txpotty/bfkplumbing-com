    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Louisville, KY

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kentucky homeowners.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Louisville

    BFK Plumbing uses industry-leading trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Louisville is not only low impact but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Pipe Bursting in Louisville, KY

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Louisville

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.
