    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Louisville

    Our team is skilled in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Louisville due to their quiet operation and compact design.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Louisville, KY

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Louisville, KY.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Louisville, KY

    For Wayne sump pump owners in Louisville, KY, BFK Plumbing offers professional maintenance.     Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Louisville

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to confirm it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Louisville home from unexpected flooding.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Louisville

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacement every three to five years.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Louisville, KY

We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Hard freezes happen most winters in Kentucky, and pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages are the most at risk.
