    PEX Pipe Replacement in Louisville, KY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. BFK Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Louisville, KY, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Louisville, KY

    Much of Kentucky sits on limestone, so water tends to run hard, leaving scale in water heaters and fixtures over time. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Louisville

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Cost-Effective Repiping Comparisons in Louisville, KY

    Understanding the cost of repiping is crucial for homeowners in Louisville, KY. We provide clear comparisons between PEX and copper repiping options, detailing material costs, labor, and expected lifespan. PEX is generally more affordable for materials and installation, while copper offers a premium, long-lasting solution with excellent resale value. Our estimates include all necessary permits, fittings, and cleanup, ensuring you have a clear picture of your investment before any work begins, with no hidden fees.

    Copper Repiping in Louisville, KY

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.
