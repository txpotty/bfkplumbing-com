    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Louisville, KY

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.
