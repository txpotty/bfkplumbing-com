    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Louisville, KY

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, usually located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Kentucky ordinances for waste management.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Louisville, KY

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Louisville, KY. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to improve separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Louisville

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running well.
