    Local Shower Repair Services in Louisville, KY

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Louisville, KY

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Shower Drain Repair in Louisville

    Slow shower drains are usually clogged with hair and soap scum, often hardened by mineral deposits. We clear the drain with augers and check the drain assembly for leaks. Loose or cracked drain bodies can let water seep under the pan. We repair or replace drain components when needed.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Louisville, KY

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Louisville

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed. We use advanced diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact location of the leak and provide precise repairs, often involving O-ring replacement or tightening connections, preventing costly water damage to your home's structure.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Louisville, KY

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventative measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Louisville, KY

    Many homes in Louisville feature Moen or Delta shower fixtures, known for their quality but still susceptible to wear and tear. Our plumbers are very experienced in repairing and sourcing parts for these popular brands. Whether it is a leaky single-handle cartridge or a diverter issue, we have the specialized knowledge to fix your Moen or Delta shower correctly.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Louisville

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. If you cannot get enough hot water, the anti-scald limit stop may be set too low or the cartridge may be worn. We repair or replace pressure-balance and thermostatic valves and adjust limit stops. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Louisville, KY

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.
