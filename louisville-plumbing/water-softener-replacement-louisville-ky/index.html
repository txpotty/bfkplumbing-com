    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Louisville

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Louisville, KY

    For homeowners in Louisville, KY looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Louisville

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Louisville

    For homeowners in Louisville, hard water can lead to scale buildup in pipes, shorter appliance lifespan and dry skin. Our residential water softener replacement service involves removing your old, inefficient unit and installing a new, high-efficiency system designed to meet your family's needs. We ensure proper sizing and setup for strong performance, protecting your home's plumbing.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Louisville, KY

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Louisville

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is more effective for soap and fixtures.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is generally not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be necessary. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.
