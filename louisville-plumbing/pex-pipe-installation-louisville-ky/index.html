    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Louisville

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Louisville can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Louisville, KY

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water.     Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Louisville

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or fully covered.

    Most whole-home PEX repipes take two to four days. Larger homes or difficult attic access can take a bit longer.

    The timeline for PEX pipe installation depends on the size and complexity of the project. A small repair or extension might take a few hours, while a complete whole-home repipe for an average-sized house in Louisville usually takes 2 to 5 days. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and rejected inspections.

    We make small, targeted openings to run new pipe and patch them when done. Most homeowners are surprised by how little drywall work is needed.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but prone to pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.
