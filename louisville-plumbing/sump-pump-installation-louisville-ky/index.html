    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.     High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Louisville

    A high water alarm lets you know when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails.     New Sump Pit Installation in Louisville

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.
