    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Henderson, KY

    Power outages during heavy storms are a serious concern, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition.     New Sump Pit Installation in Henderson

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Henderson, KY

    Many older homes in Henderson, KY have crawl spaces that are prone to moisture issues and standing water.     Sump Pump Replacement in Henderson, KY

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before the rainy season is a smart move.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Henderson

    A high water alarm alerts you when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are particularly useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Henderson

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Henderson

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, ideal for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.
