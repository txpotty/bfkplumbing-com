    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Henderson

    The cost of grease trap installation in Henderson, KY varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, plan on between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are typically placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work. We aim to complete installations efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes. Attempting a DIY installation can lead to costly errors, code violations, and potential health hazards. It is always best to hire licensed and qualified plumbers like BFK Plumbing.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent sewer backups, persistent foul odors coming from drains, slow drainage throughout your kitchen, or if your current unit is visibly corroded or leaking. If your existing trap is old and no longer meets current local codes in Henderson, an upgrade is also a good idea.
