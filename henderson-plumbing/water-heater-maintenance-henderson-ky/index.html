    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Henderson

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Henderson

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Henderson

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Kentucky has humid summers and changeable winters, with frequent freeze and thaw cycles that put stress on pipes and fittings. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Henderson

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Henderson, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
