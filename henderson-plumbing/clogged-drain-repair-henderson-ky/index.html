    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Henderson

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain usually costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line typically costs $350 to $800.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in diagnosing and clearing various types of drain clogs, supporting a high standard of service.

    Enzyme or bacterial cleaners can help reduce organic buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains over time. They are gentler than caustic products and safe for most pipes. They will not clear a full clog but can help head off one.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or significant mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, BFK Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Henderson. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run past the usual time.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up. These symptoms often point to a deeper, more serious issue in your main sewer line that requires expert diagnosis and specialized tools.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the difficulty.
