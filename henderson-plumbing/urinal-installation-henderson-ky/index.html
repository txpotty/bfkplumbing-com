    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Henderson

    Replacing a urinal on existing lines usually costs $500 to $1,200 including a manual flush valve. New installs with carriers and new drain lines usually range from $1,500 to $3,500, and sensor valves add $250 to $600.

    Yes, it is possible to install a urinal in a home in Henderson, though it's less common than in commercial settings. Considerations include available space, existing plumbing, and ventilation. Our plumbers can assess your home to determine feasibility and recommend suitable residential models.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water. Waterless urinals offer major water savings but require specific maintenance for their sealant cartridges. Both types offer effective waste disposal.

    While new urinal installations are usually scheduled, we understand that commercial needs can sometimes be urgent, especially during renovations. Contact us at (502) 300-9896 to discuss your timeline; we'll do our best to handle urgent requests for businesses in Henderson.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more strongly down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    New urinals and restroom changes generally require a plumbing permit. Simple like-for-like replacements usually do not, and we will confirm with your local building department.

    Most standard urinal installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. However, if new plumbing lines need to be run, or if there are significant modifications to the existing wall or drainage system, the process could take longer, possibly a full day.
