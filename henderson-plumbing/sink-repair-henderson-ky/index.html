    Sink Trap and P-Trap Repair in Henderson, KY

    The P-trap under your sink is crucial for preventing sewer gases from entering your home and catching items that fall down the drain. If it's leaking, corroded, or improperly installed, it can cause significant problems. We provide professional repair and replacement of P-traps, making sure of a proper seal and function for your sink plumbing in Henderson, KY.

    Leaky Sink Faucet Repair in Henderson

    A constant drip from your sink faucet can waste gallons of water and lead to higher utility bills. Our technicians in Henderson specialize in diagnosing and repairing leaky faucets, often by replacing worn-out washers, O-rings, or cartridges. We work with all major brands like Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard, ensuring a precise fit and lasting seal to stop those annoying drips.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Henderson, KY

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Henderson

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Sink Repair vs Replacement Costs in Henderson, KY

    A trap, stopper or supply line repair typically runs a fraction of the cost of a new sink. Replacement starts to make sense when the basin is cracked, the finish is badly etched, or the drain body is corroded into the bowl. We give you both prices when it is close so you can decide. Many customers save money by repairing now and planning an upgrade later.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Henderson

    Kitchen sinks endure heavy use, leading to common issues like garbage disposal clogs, leaky basket strainers, or worn-out sprayer hoses. BFK Plumbing specializes in diagnosing these specific problems, whether it's a cracked basin or a faulty P-trap. We can repair or replace components from brands like Moen, Delta, and Kohler, ensuring your kitchen sink is fully functional and ready for daily demands. Our repairs are built to last, handling the tough demands of cooking and cleaning.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Henderson

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you honestly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Henderson, KY

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that heads off a very expensive one.
