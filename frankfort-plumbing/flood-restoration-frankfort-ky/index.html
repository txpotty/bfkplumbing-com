    Frequently Asked Questions About Flood Restoration in Frankfort

    The cost of flood restoration in Frankfort, KY can vary significantly depending on the extent of the damage, the size of the affected area, and the type of water, whether clean, gray, or black water. Minor water damage might range from $1,500 to $4,000, while extensive flooding requiring structural drying and repairs could cost upwards of $10,000 to $30,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates.

    Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flooding from outside surface water. Separate flood insurance is typically needed for storm runoff. Damage from a burst pipe or appliance failure is more often covered.

    We use professional-grade equipment from leading manufacturers in the restoration industry. This includes powerful water extraction units from brands like Prochem and Mytee, high-capacity dehumidifiers from Dri-Eaz and Abatement Technologies, and air movers from trusted makers. We also use professional moisture detection tools from Flir and Tramex.

    Signs of hidden water damage include musty odors, peeling paint or wallpaper, visible mold spots, warped flooring, or discolored walls or ceilings. If you suspect hidden moisture, our thermal imaging and moisture detection tools can locate affected areas that are not visible to the naked eye.

    Stay safe and avoid electrical hazards. If possible, shut off power and water to affected areas and take photos of the damage. Then call a restoration crew to start water extraction.

    Permits are usually not required for basic water extraction and drying. However, if the flood damage calls for major structural repairs, electrical work, or plumbing system overhauls, permits from Frankfort authorities may be required. BFK Plumbing will advise you on any required permits and can help coordinate the application process.

    Drywall and flooring replacement usually do not need permits, but water heater replacement, electrical repairs and structural work often do. We handle plumbing permits when needed.

    The duration of flood restoration varies based on the severity of the damage. Initial water extraction might take a few hours, but the drying process often requires 3 to 5 days, depending on humidity and the amount of moisture. Complete restoration, including repairs, can extend to several weeks or more for serious cases.
