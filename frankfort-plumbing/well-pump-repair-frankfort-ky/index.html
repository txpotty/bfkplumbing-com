    Jet Pump System Repair in Frankfort, KY

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. BFK Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Frankfort operates at peak efficiency.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Frankfort, KY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Spring storms can raise groundwater quickly, making sump pump checks before the rainy season a good habit. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.
