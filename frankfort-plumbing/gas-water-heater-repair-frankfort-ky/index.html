    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Frankfort

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Frankfort

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Frankfort are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Frankfort, KY

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We will provide an honest assessment of the repair options for your Frankfort, KY home.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Frankfort

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Frankfort

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.
