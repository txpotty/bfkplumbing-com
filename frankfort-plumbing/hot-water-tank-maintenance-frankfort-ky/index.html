    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Frankfort, KY

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Frankfort

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Frankfort home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Frankfort

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Frankfort, KY

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an straight read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running well for years.

    Whether your home in Frankfort, KY uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Frankfort

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is not unusual in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Frankfort

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, preventing unexpected cold showers for your family in Frankfort.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Frankfort, KY

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.
