    Long-Lasting Pipe Replacement Services in Frankfort, KY

    PEX Repiping in Frankfort

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Frankfort, KY

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Frankfort, KY

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Frankfort

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Frankfort

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a popular choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Frankfort due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Frankfort

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. BFK Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials.
