    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit.     Simple replacements usually do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Elizabethtown.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by BFK Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.
