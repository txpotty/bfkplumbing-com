    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Florence

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Florence

    The cost of grease trap installation in Florence, KY varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation.     We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps.     The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours.     Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours.     Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also warranty our installation workmanship and connections.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks.
