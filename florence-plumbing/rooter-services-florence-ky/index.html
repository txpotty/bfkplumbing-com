    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    While chemical drain cleaners are available, BFK Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and carry safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.
