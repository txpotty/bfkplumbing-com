    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Florence, KY

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Much of Kentucky sits on limestone, so water tends to run moderately hard to hard, leaving scale in water heaters and fixtures over time. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
