    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Florence

    The cost of leak detection in Florence can range from $250 to $800, depending on the complexity and location of the leak. Factors like slab leaks or those requiring specialized equipment may be at the higher end. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial assessment.

    Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material breakdown. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are usually more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    While you can check for obvious leaks, hidden leaks often require professional equipment like acoustic sensors, thermal cameras or tracer gas to locate precisely. A professional plumber will pinpoint the source without unnecessary damage to your home, saving you time and money.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation. Clay soil over rock is common, and it holds water against foundations, so good drainage and sump pumps matter for many homes. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on plumbing lines, and temperature swings cause pipes to expand and contract over the years.

    Older galvanized steel pipes are prone to corrosion, and some older polybutylene pipes are known for early failure. However, even modern materials like copper or PEX can leak due to improper installation, high water pressure or foundation movement.

    Common signs include a sudden spike in your water bill, damp spots on walls or floors, mildew odors, visible mold growth, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, or a continuously running water meter even when all water is off.
