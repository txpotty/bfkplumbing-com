    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Florence, KY

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Florence and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Florence, KY

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is usually the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line out to the main.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Florence, KY

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Florence, KY while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Florence, KY

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Florence

This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.
