    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Richmond, KY

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Richmond

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Richmond

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to identify grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can clear out stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Richmond

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil over rock is common, and it holds water against foundations, so drainage and sump pumps matter for many homes. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and costly emergency repairs.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Richmond

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.
