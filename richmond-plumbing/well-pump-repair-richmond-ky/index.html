    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Richmond

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Richmond.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Richmond, KY

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. BFK Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Richmond operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Richmond

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
