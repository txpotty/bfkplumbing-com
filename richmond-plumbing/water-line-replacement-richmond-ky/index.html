    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Richmond

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and chokes the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Richmond

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. BFK Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a reliable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Richmond, KY.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Richmond, KY

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is preferred by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to cushion it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Richmond

    The main water line is the primary pipe carrying water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most sensible long-term solution. We expertly excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Richmond, KY

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Most Kentucky homeowners disconnect hoses and protect outdoor spigots in the fall, and many insulate vulnerable pipes in crawlspaces. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Richmond. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Richmond

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Richmond. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Richmond, KY

    For homeowners looking to minimize disruption to their landscaping, trenchless water line replacement offers an effective alternative. Using pipe bursting or pipe pulling techniques, we can install a new water line with minimal excavation, typically requiring only two small access points. This method is well suited for properties in Richmond with mature trees, detailed landscaping, or concrete driveways, saving time and restoration costs.
