    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Richmond, KY

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Private wells and cisterns still serve some rural homes, and iron, sulfur, and sediment are frequent reasons for water treatment. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Richmond

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost.
