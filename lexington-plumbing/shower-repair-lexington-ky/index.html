    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Lexington, KY

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed fast.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Lexington, KY

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower. We provide effective solutions to restore consistent hot water flow and pressure.

    Low Water Pressure in Shower Repair in Lexington, KY

    Experiencing weak water flow in your shower can be annoying. Low water pressure can be caused by various issues, including mineral buildup in the shower head, a faulty pressure balancing valve, or even problems with your home's main water line. We diagnose the specific cause and implement targeted repairs to restore strong, consistent water pressure to your shower in Lexington.

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Lexington

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn smoothly again.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Lexington

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers promptly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will ensure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Lexington, KY

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Clogged Shower Drain Solutions in Lexington, KY

    Slow or standing water in your shower is a clear sign of a clogged drain. These clogs are typically caused by a buildup of hair, soap scum, and sometimes mineral deposits. Our team uses professional drain snakes and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the most stubborn blockages without damaging your pipes. We also offer advice on preventive measures to keep your shower drains flowing freely.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Lexington

    A malfunctioning showerhead can spray erratically, have reduced pressure, or even leak. Often, mineral deposits from the water supply can clog the nozzles, or internal components can wear out. We can clean, repair, or replace your showerhead with a new, efficient model. We offer options from standard to rain showerheads, providing proper installation for strong performance and water conservation.
