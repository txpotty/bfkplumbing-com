    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Lexington

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Lexington

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Lexington. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Lexington

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. BFK Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and resist bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a reliable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Lexington, KY.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Lexington, KY

    For homeowners looking to minimize disruption to their landscaping, trenchless water line replacement offers an excellent alternative.
