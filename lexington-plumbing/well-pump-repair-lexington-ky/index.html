    Jet Pump System Repair in Lexington, KY

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. BFK Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Lexington operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Lexington, KY

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Heavy rain and flash flooding are common in hilly parts of Kentucky, where runoff can swamp drains and push water into crawlspaces. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Lexington

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Lexington, KY

    Many well pump failures stem from electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components.     Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Lexington, KY

    Many well pump issues come from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also adept at electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults.     Pressure Tank Repair in Lexington, KY

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Repair in Lexington

    The cost of well pump repair in Lexington can vary significantly, typically ranging from $300 for minor repairs like a pressure switch replacement to over $2,000 for complex issues such as replacing a submersible pump motor. Factors include the type of pump, the depth of the well, and the exact parts needed. We provide a clear estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    BFK Plumbing services and repairs all major brands of well pumps commonly found in Kentucky, including Franklin Electric, Grundfos, Sta-Rite, Myers, Goulds, and Little Giant. Our technicians are trained on the specifics of each manufacturer's products, ensuring competent repairs for your system in Lexington.

    Well pump failures can be caused by several factors such as electrical problems (power surges, faulty wiring), mechanical wear and tear on pump components, sediment buildup in the well, or issues with the pressure tank or switch. Age and consistent use also contribute.

    Submersible pumps commonly last 8 to 15 years, depending on run time, sand and water quality. Short cycling from a bad pressure tank can shorten that a lot.

    Watch for sputtering faucets, low or fluctuating pressure, a pump that runs constantly, air in the lines or sudden spikes in your electric bill. A constantly clicking pressure switch also signals trouble.

    Most pump replacements take four to eight hours once parts are on site. Very deep wells or stuck pumps can take more time.

    Common signs include no water, low water pressure, intermittent water flow, strange noises coming from the pump or pressure tank, cloudy or rusty water, or your pump running constantly. If you notice any of these symptoms in your Lexington home, it's best to call a professional quickly.

    The length of a well pump repair depends on the issue. Simple fixes like a pressure switch replacement might take a few hours, while replacing a submersible pump can take a full day, especially if the well is deep. We work for efficient service to restore your water as quickly as possible.
