    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Lexington, KY

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kentucky homeowners.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Lexington

    Before any trenchless repair, we perform a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This crucial step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Lexington property, avoiding guesswork.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Lexington

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Lexington that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Lexington, KY

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be used to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Lexington, KY. This preserves your home's interior and reduces renovation costs.

    Pipe Bursting in Lexington, KY

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Lexington, KY

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.
