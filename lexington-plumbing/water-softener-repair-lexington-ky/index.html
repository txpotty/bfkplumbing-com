    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Lexington, KY

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Lexington, KY

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. BFK Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these unique units in Lexington, KY, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Lexington, KY

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Lexington, KY.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Lexington

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention. BFK Plumbing offers comprehensive diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan tailored to your home in Lexington.

    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Lexington

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Lexington, KY

    Businesses in Lexington, KY rely on soft water for various operations, from laundromats and restaurants to manufacturing facilities. BFK Plumbing provides expert repair services for commercial-grade water softeners, understanding the larger scale and specialized components involved.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Lexington

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Lexington, KY

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.
