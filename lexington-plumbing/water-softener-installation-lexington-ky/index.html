    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Lexington

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Softener Loop Installation in Lexington

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Lexington

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing healthy minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Lexington households.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Lexington

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Lexington

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Lexington home. This ensures every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including needed bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Water Softener Replacement in Lexington, KY

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Lexington, KY

    BFK Plumbing is experienced in installing Fleck water softener systems, a trusted name in water treatment. Fleck control valves are known for their durability and reliability, offering several programming options to optimize performance. We ensure your Fleck system is properly configured for the water conditions in Lexington, KY.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Lexington, KY

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin. We install combination systems with filter and softener tanks or dual-stage units. This setup improves both water quality and feel. Many homeowners notice a real difference in taste.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Lexington

    The cost of water softener installation in Lexington, KY usually ranges from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the system type, brand and complexity of the installation. Factors like existing plumbing modifications and the size of your home can influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after an on-site assessment.

    We offer a warranty on our workmanship, and the water softener units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, which usually range from 5 to 10 years for the tank and control valve. We will provide you with all warranty details for your particular system after installation.

    Control valves typically carry 5 years of coverage, and tanks often carry 10 years or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    With proper maintenance, a quality water softener system can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. The lifespan depends on the quality of the unit, the hardness of your water and how well it is maintained. Regular servicing can help lengthen its life.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more effective for very hard water.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and reduced water heater performance are common signs. A water test confirms hardness levels.

    Installing a water softener offers numerous benefits, including preventing scale buildup in pipes and appliances, extending appliance lifespan, reducing soap and detergent usage and improving the feel of your skin and hair. It also makes cleaning easier and keeps clothes looking brighter.

    Maintenance mainly involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness. It's also good practice to schedule annual check-ups with a professional plumber to ensure the system is operating efficiently and to address any potential issues.
