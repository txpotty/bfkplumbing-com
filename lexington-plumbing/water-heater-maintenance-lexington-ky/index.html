    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Lexington

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup.     Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Lexington, KY

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Lexington, KY

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in Lexington, KY. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Lexington, KY

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
