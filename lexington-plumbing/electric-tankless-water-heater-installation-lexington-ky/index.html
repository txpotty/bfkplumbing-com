    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Much of Kentucky sits on limestone, so water tends to run moderately hard to hard, leaving scale in water heaters and fixtures over time. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
