    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Lexington, KY

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Lexington, KY, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Lexington

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Lexington, KY

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. BFK Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Lexington, KY so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Lexington

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too often or failing under stress, common issues in some Lexington properties.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Lexington

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with sufficient run-time to protect your Lexington home through extended power interruptions. You get peace of mind when the lights go out.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Lexington

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Lexington for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Lexington, KY

    For maximum protection, many homeowners in Lexington choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. BFK Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Lexington, KY

BFK Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Lexington

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Lexington, KY can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation.     It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.
