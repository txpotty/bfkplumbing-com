    Professional Gas Water Heater Repair Services in Radcliff, KY

    Pilot Light Repair in Radcliff

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Radcliff

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Radcliff

    Many homes in Radcliff have older gas water heater models that require specialized knowledge for repair. Our technicians are familiar with the common issues and less common parts associated with these systems. We can troubleshoot and repair older units, often extending their lifespan, rather than pushing for instant replacement. We focus on cost-effective repairs that respect the character of your home.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Radcliff, KY

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Radcliff

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is vital for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Radcliff home. Proper venting is a critical safety consideration.

    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Serviced in Radcliff

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Radcliff

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.
