    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion.     Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
