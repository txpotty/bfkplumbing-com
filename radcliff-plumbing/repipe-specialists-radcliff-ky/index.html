    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Radcliff

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Kentucky has karst terrain with limestone bedrock and sinkholes, which can slow down sewer line and drainage work. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
