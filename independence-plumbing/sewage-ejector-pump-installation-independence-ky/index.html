    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Independence

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. BFK Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Independence. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.
