    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Independence

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard.     Sump Pump Maintenance in Independence, KY

    Heavy rain and flash flooding are common in hilly parts of Kentucky, where runoff can overwhelm drains and push water into crawlspaces. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Independence.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Independence

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.
