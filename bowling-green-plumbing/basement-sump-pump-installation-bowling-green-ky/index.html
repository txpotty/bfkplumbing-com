    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Bowling Green

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bowling Green, KY

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Bowling Green, KY. Heavy rain and flash flooding are common in hilly parts of Kentucky, where runoff can swamp drains and push water into crawlspaces.     Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Bowling Green

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Bowling Green property.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Bowling Green

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.
