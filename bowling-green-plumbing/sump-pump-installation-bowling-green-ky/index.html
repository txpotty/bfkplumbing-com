    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Bowling Green, KY

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.     Sump Pump Replacement in Bowling Green, KY

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Bowling Green

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This confirms the work meets local building and plumbing codes. BFK Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Bowling Green, KY to ensure your installation is fully compliant.

    We install Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and other dependable brands. We recommend models based on your pit size and water inflow.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned annually, especially before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to confirm it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent unexpected failures.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.
