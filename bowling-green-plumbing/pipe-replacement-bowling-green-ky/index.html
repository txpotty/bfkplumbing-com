    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Bowling Green

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s.     Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Bowling Green, KY

    Hard freezes happen most winters in Kentucky, and pipes in crawlspaces, exterior walls, and unheated garages are the first to freeze. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Bowling Green, KY

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
