    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Bowling Green

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Bowling Green

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an excellent option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are especially well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. BFK Plumbing carefully replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Bowling Green

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Bowling Green provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bowling Green, KY

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Bowling Green, KY

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they eliminate pressure swings.
