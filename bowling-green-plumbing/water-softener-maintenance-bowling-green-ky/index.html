    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Bowling Green, KY

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Bowling Green

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Bowling Green, KY

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Bowling Green homes.
