    The cost of PEX pipe installation in Covington can range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, accessibility and whether it is a full repipe or a partial replacement.     PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Summer humidity often causes sweating cold-water pipes and damp crawlspaces, which can encourage mold and wood rot. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Covington.

    Yes, PEX piping is certified safe for drinking water. It does not leach chemicals into water and is approved by all major plumbing codes for potable water systems. It is a cleaner alternative to older pipes that can corrode and affect water quality.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.
