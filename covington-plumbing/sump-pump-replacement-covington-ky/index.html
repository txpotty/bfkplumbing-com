    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Covington, KY

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Covington

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Covington, KY

    For added protection, many homeowners in Covington choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. BFK Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Covington

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Covington

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Covington

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Covington, KY typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are our pick for durability.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are common signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    Simple replacements usually do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by BFK Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.
