    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Covington, KY

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Covington, KY

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Covington, KY

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Covington, KY

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Covington
