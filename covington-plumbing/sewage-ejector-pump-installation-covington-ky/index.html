    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Covington, KY

    The basin is a critical component of any sewage ejector pump system. BFK Plumbing provides professional sewage ejector pump basin installation in Covington, KY, making sure it is properly sealed, vented and sized for your pump. An incorrectly installed or undersized basin can lead to frequent pump cycling, early wear and even overflows. We use durable, corrosion-resistant basins and make sure all connections are watertight and gas-tight to prevent odors and health hazards in your home or business.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Covington, KY

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option. A new system with basin, floor excavation, venting and electrical costs more but enables a full below-grade bathroom. Macerating toilets often cost less upfront but have more limitations. We give you a detailed quote so you can compare a standard ejector, grinder pump and upflush system together.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Covington

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so toilets, showers and sinks drain into a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We excavate the floor, set a basin usually 30 to 50 gallons and connect each fixture drain. A solids-handling pump lifts waste up and into the main drain line. The basin lid is sealed and vented to keep odors out.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Covington

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet and pumps waste through small-diameter pipe up to the drain line. They work well for small basement bathrooms, laundry rooms and guest suites. We explain their limits, including what should never be flushed, so the system stays reliable.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Covington

    Where wastewater contains tougher solids, a grinder pump installation in Covington might be necessary. Unlike standard ejector pumps, grinder pumps macerate waste into a fine slurry before pumping it to the sewer line. This is often required for certain commercial applications or residential setups with specific waste disposal needs. BFK Plumbing can help you determine if a grinder pump is the right solution, handling the whole installation from start to finish, including the specialized electrical and discharge piping.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Covington, KY

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and homes with heavy below-grade use often need duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. If one pump fails, the other keeps working while an alarm notifies staff. We size the basin and pumps for peak flow and install controls to code. Maintenance schedules are provided to keep both pumps in good condition.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Covington, KY

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer line. A smaller ejector or laundry tray pump can handle washer discharge and sink drainage. We install a lint filter where needed to protect the pump. These systems are compact and can usually be installed without major floor demolition.
