    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Covington

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down tough blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines.     Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Covington

    Beyond the trap itself, the drain lines leading to and from the grease trap can also suffer damage or blockages. These lines are especially susceptible to grease build-up. In Covington, we diagnose and repair issues within these connecting pipes, including clearing FOG accumulation, fixing collapsed sections, or repairing root intrusions.     Grease Trap Leak Repair in Covington

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we suggest replacement.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Covington

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Covington

    The most common grease trap issue is a severe clog caused by accumulated fats, oils, and grease. Our plumbers in Covington use specialized hydro-jetting equipment to effectively break down and flush out stubborn blockages from grease traps and their connecting drain lines. This method is very effective for restoring full flow and preventing immediate recurrence, much more so than chemical drain cleaners which can damage pipes.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Covington

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.
