    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Covington

    The cost of water heater replacement in Covington, KY typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity affect the final price.

    BFK Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size based on your bathroom count and usage.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Private wells and cisterns still serve some rural homes, and iron, sulfur, and sediment are frequent reasons for water treatment. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. BFK Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
