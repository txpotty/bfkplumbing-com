    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Covington

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Covington

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Covington

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Covington, KY

    River flooding and saturated ground can cause sewage backups, so backwater valves and sump pumps are worthwhile upgrades. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Covington.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Covington, KY

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use advanced tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Kentucky has many older homes with cast iron drains, galvanized supply pipes, and clay sewer laterals nearing the end of their life. Catching these leaks early prevents significant water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Covington from extensive repairs down the line.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Covington

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Covington

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Private wells and cisterns still serve some rural homes, and iron, sulfur, and grit are frequent reasons for water treatment. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, improves efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.
