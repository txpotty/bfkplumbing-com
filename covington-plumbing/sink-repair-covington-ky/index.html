    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Covington

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a properly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the quickest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Covington, KY
