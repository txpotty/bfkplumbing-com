    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Covington

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Covington, KY

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in Covington, KY may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps. These units are well suited for capturing FOG at the source, preventing it from entering the main plumbing lines. Our team expertly installs these smaller units, ensuring proper sizing, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system for effective waste management and odor control.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Covington

    Small indoor hydromechanical traps typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed. Large outdoor gravity interceptors often run $8,000 to $30,000 or more with excavation, concrete and site restoration. We provide a written estimate after a site visit.

    BFK Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will usually come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring peace of mind.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are usually placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    We recommend and install high-quality, durable grease trap brands such as Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. These manufacturers are known for their reliability and compliance with industry standards, offering various solutions to meet different commercial kitchen needs.

    Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours. Our experienced plumbers will conduct a complete assessment of your Covington business to recommend the appropriate size and type of grease trap that meets local codes and your operational needs.

    Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also warranty our installation workmanship and connections.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are usually rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work.
