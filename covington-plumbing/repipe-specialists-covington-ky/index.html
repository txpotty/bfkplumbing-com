    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Covington, KY

    Much of Kentucky sits on limestone, so water tends to run hard, leaving scale in water heaters and fixtures over time. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, ensuring a longer-lasting plumbing system.
