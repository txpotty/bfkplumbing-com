    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Covington

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines do best with both.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a far more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Covington. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be needed. We will handle all necessary permitting if that situation arises.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows damage.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at BFK Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already weakened, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.

    For most standard drain cleaning or rooter services within your property's existing plumbing system, permits are generally not required in Covington, KY.
