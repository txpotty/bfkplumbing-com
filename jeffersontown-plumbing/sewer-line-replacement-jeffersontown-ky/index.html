    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Jeffersontown, KY

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Jeffersontown, KY while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Jeffersontown

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.
