    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Jeffersontown, KY

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Jeffersontown, KY

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the cause. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is frequently paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    Water Line Installation Cost Comparison in Jeffersontown, KY

    Cost depends largely on run length, soil conditions and pipe material. A short PEX run through soft soil is the most affordable option, while long copper runs through rocky ground cost more in both labor and materials. Trenchless methods cost more per foot but can save thousands in concrete and landscape repair. We provide a written quote that itemizes trenching, pipe, fittings, permits and restoration.

    New Construction Water Service Lines in Jeffersontown

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a properly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load. Most homes need a 3/4 inch or 1 inch line, while larger homes with pools and big irrigation systems often benefit from 1 1/4 inch. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready when the inspector arrives.

    Water Line Replacement for Older Homes in Jeffersontown
