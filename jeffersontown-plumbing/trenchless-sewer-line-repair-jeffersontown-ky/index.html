    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Jeffersontown

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Jeffersontown, KY

    Sewer lines in Jeffersontown, KY can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Clay soil over rock is common, and it holds water against foundations, so good drainage and sump pumps matter for many homes.     Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Jeffersontown, KY

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Kentucky homeowners.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Jeffersontown

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.
