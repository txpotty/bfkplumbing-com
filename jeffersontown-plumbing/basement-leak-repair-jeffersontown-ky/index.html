    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Jeffersontown, KY

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most cases.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Jeffersontown, KY

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale with age, and plastic joints can separate if a line was poorly supported. These leaks usually show up only when an upstairs fixture runs. We run water through each drain while watching the basement, then confirm the crack with a camera. Repairs range from a single section swap to replacing an entire stack with PVC or ABS.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Jeffersontown

    A basement floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage or a failed trap. We clear the drain, check the line to the main sewer and replace cracked traps and covers. Installing a backwater valve stops sewage from pushing back into the basement if the main line surcharges. We set the valve where it can be serviced easily.
