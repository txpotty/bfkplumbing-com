    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Jeffersontown

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    We use industry-leading equipment for our rooter services. Our technicians commonly use professional-grade tools from brands like RIDGID, Spartan Tool, and Gorlitz for drain augers, sewer cameras, and hydro-jetting machines. These brands are known for their durability and performance in tough plumbing situations.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and sewage odors are common signs. Water backing up into a tub when you flush is another clue. Frequent clogs in the same line also suggest a deeper blockage.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Jeffersontown. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be required. We will handle all required permitting if that situation arises.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and throw away fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines benefit from both.
