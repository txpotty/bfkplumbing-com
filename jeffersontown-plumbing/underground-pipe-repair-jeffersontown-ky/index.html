    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends on the complexity. A minor leak repair might take a few hours, while a full trenchless sewer line replacement could take one to three days. We always aim for efficient service with minimal disruption.
