    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Jeffersontown, KY

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Jeffersontown, KY

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Jeffersontown, KY

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Water Leak Detection Cost Comparison in Jeffersontown, KY

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Jeffersontown, KY

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Jeffersontown

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes that have water softeners.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Jeffersontown

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Jeffersontown

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, BFK Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be vulnerable to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across various pipe materials, ensuring accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.
