    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Jeffersontown

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Jeffersontown

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Jeffersontown residence.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Jeffersontown

    Spring storms can lift groundwater quickly, making sump pump checks before the rainy season a good habit. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Jeffersontown, KY
