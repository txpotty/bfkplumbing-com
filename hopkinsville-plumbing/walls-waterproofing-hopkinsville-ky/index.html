    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Hopkinsville, KY

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall.     Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Hopkinsville

    Kentucky has many older homes with cast iron drains, galvanized steel supply pipes, and clay sewer laterals nearing the end of their life. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Hopkinsville

    Many homes in Hopkinsville have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Hopkinsville

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Hopkinsville

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Drainage System Integration in Hopkinsville, KY

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Hopkinsville, KY properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.
