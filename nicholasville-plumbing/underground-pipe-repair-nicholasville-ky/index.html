    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Nicholasville

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Nicholasville

    Trenchless pipe repair offers a less invasive alternative to traditional excavation for buried pipe issues.     Pipe Bursting Services in Nicholasville, KY

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a robust solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Nicholasville, KY.
