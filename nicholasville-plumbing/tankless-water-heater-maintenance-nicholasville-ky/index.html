    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Nicholasville

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Nicholasville is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, providing recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Nicholasville

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Commercial Tankless System Maintenance in Nicholasville, KY

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Nicholasville

    Proper ventilation is critical for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Nicholasville includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We make sure that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Nicholasville, KY

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Nicholasville

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Nicholasville
