    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Nicholasville

    Most shower repairs, like cartridge replacement or diverter repair, cost $150 to $375. Valve replacements that require opening the wall usually run $450 to $1,200. Shower pan repairs can cost more depending on the damage.

    Many brands, including Moen and Delta, offer lifetime cartridge warranties to the original owner. We can help you check whether your parts are covered.

    Leaks behind the wall are often due to a faulty shower valve, loose pipe connections, or cracks in the shower pan or tile grout. These leaks can be serious, leading to mold growth and structural damage. Our team uses specialized detection equipment to pinpoint the leak source and repair it with minimal wall disruption.

    A dripping shower typically has a worn cartridge or seals in the valve. Hard water speeds up wear, so drips are common in many homes.

    Start by cleaning the showerhead to remove mineral buildup. If pressure stays low, the cartridge, flow restrictor or a pressure regulator may need service.

    Key signs include a dripping faucet, low water pressure, inconsistent water temperature, standing water in the shower, strange noises from the pipes, or visible leaks around the shower base. Addressing these signs early can prevent more serious damage.

    While some minor issues like a clogged showerhead might be DIY-friendly, most shower repairs, especially those involving water pressure, valve replacement, or significant leaks, are best left to a professional.
