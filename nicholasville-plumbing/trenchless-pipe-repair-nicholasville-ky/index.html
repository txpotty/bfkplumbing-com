    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Nicholasville, KY

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Nicholasville

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Nicholasville.

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter.
